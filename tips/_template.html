How can I use AI to plan a week of family dinners in 5 minutes?

By Adam Hunter · Issue #1 · October 27, 2026 · 3-minute read

Short answer: Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok with your family's preferences, and it will write five weeknight dinners plus one grocery list sorted by aisle. It takes about five minutes, and you can ask it to swap any meal your kids won't eat.

How to do it

  1. Open ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok. The free version is fine.
  2. Copy the prompt below and fill in the parts in [brackets].
  3. Read the plan and reply with swaps, like "no fish on Wednesday."
  4. Ask: "Now give me the grocery list grouped by store section."
Copy-paste prompt 
You are a practical family meal planner. Plan 5 weeknight dinners for a family of [number] with kids aged [ages].
Rules: each dinner takes 30 minutes or less, uses [allergies/dislikes] as hard limits, and reuses ingredients across meals to cut waste.
Then give me one grocery list grouped by store section, with quantities.

Double-check: AI can get allergens and cooking temperatures wrong. Always check labels and cook meat to safe temperatures.

FAQ

Do I need the paid version of ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok?

No. This prompt works in the free versions. Paid versions just handle longer lists better.

Is it safe to share my family's details?

Share food preferences and allergies, not names, schools, or addresses. The AI doesn't need them.

What if the AI suggests something my kids won't eat?

Reply with "swap Tuesday for something without mushrooms" and it will redo just that day.
