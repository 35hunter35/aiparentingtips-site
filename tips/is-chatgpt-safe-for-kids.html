Is ChatGPT safe for kids? A parent's simple guide

By Adam Hunter · October 17, 2026 · 3-minute read

Short answer: ChatGPT isn't designed for young kids. OpenAI requires users to be 13 or older, with a parent's permission under 18, and Claude requires users to be 18+. If your child is under 13, you do the typing on your own account and talk about the answers together, or use an option made for kids' accounts, like Gemini through Google Family Link. For any age, set simple family rules, and the prompt below helps you write them.

Copy-paste prompt (paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or Gemini) 
Help me write simple family rules for using AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.
My kids are ages [ages]. We use AI for [homework help / stories / curiosity questions].
Please write:
1. 6-8 short rules a [youngest age]-year-old can understand, covering:
   - never sharing full name, school, address, photos, or passwords
   - AI can be wrong, so double-check facts
   - telling a parent if anything feels weird or upsetting
   - when and where AI is allowed (for example, only in the kitchen with a parent)
   - homework: AI can give hints, not do the work
2. A short version of the rules for a fridge poster.
3. 3 conversation starters I can use to talk with my kids about AI.

How to do it

  1. Check the age rules for the tool your kid wants to use.
  2. Use AI together first. Sit with younger kids and look at what they ask.
  3. Paste the prompt below to draft your family's AI rules.
  4. Print the rules and revisit them every few months as your kids grow.

Good to know: Age rules differ by app. ChatGPT and Grok are 13+, with a parent's permission under 18. Meta AI, Snapchat's My AI, and Microsoft Copilot are 13+. Claude is 18+, and Character.AI no longer lets under-18s chat with characters. Parents can turn on Gemini for kids under 13 through Google Family Link. Rules change, so check the app's own terms first. Parent tools to look for: ChatGPT parental controls, Google Family Link, Meta AI supervision, Snapchat Family Center, and Microsoft Family Safety.

Don't rely on parental alerts alone: In October 2026, NPR reported that Common Sense Media found ChatGPT's teen parental alerts unreliable in crisis situations (OpenAI disputes how the test was run). Alerts can help, but keep talking with your kids about how they use AI. If your child is in crisis, call or text 988 (US).

Safety first: Don't type your kids' full names, school, address, or photos into any AI tool. It doesn't need them. Most AI chatbots are for ages 13 and up (ChatGPT and Grok also need a parent's permission under 18), and Claude is 18+, so check each tool's age rules before a child uses it.

FAQ

What age can kids use ChatGPT?

OpenAI's terms say users must be at least 13, and under-18 users need a parent's or guardian's permission. Check the current terms, since rules can change.

Is Claude or Gemini safer for kids?

Claude is for adults only (18+). Google lets parents turn on the Gemini app for kids under 13 through a supervised Family Link account, though it's still rolling out and isn't offered in the EU or UK. Whatever tool you choose, sitting with your kid and setting family rules matter more than the brand.

What should kids never type into AI?

Their full name, school, address, phone number, photos of themselves, and passwords. The AI doesn't need any of it to help.

Can AI give kids wrong or inappropriate answers?

Yes. AI can make up facts and occasionally produce content that isn't right for kids. That's why using it together and talking about what you see matters.
