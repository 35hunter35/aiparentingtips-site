Is ChatGPT safe for kids? A parent's simple guide

By Adam Hunter · October 17, 2026 · 3-minute read

Short answer: ChatGPT isn't designed for young kids. OpenAI requires users to be 13 or older, with a parent's permission under 18, and Claude requires users to be 18+. Under 13, the safest approach is using AI together on your account. For any age, set simple family rules, and the prompt below helps you write them.

Copy-paste prompt (works in free ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini) 
Help me write simple family rules for using AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.
My kids are ages [ages]. We use AI for [homework help / stories / curiosity questions].
Please write:
1. 6-8 short rules a [youngest age]-year-old can understand, covering:
   - never sharing full name, school, address, photos, or passwords
   - AI can be wrong, so double-check facts
   - telling a parent if anything feels weird or upsetting
   - when and where AI is allowed (for example, only in the kitchen with a parent)
   - homework: AI can give hints, not do the work
2. A short version of the rules for a fridge poster.
3. 3 conversation starters I can use to talk with my kids about AI.

How to do it

  1. Check the age rules for the tool your kid wants to use.
  2. Use AI together first. Sit with younger kids and look at what they ask.
  3. Paste the prompt below to draft your family's AI rules.
  4. Print the rules and revisit them every few months as your kids grow.

Good to know: Most AI chatbots require users to be at least 13 (with a parent's permission under 18), and Claude requires users to be 18 or older, so check each tool's age rules before a child uses it. Some tools also offer parental controls or teen settings, so look in each app's settings.

Safety first: Don't type your kids' full names, school, address, or photos into any AI tool. It doesn't need them. FAQ

What age can kids use ChatGPT?

OpenAI's terms say users must be at least 13, and under-18 users need a parent's or guardian's permission. Check the current terms, since rules can change.

Is Claude or Gemini safer for kids?

Claude requires users to be 18 or older. Google offers some Gemini options for younger users through family accounts. Whatever tool you choose, supervising and setting family rules matters more than the brand.

What should kids never type into AI?

Their full name, school, address, phone number, photos of themselves, and passwords. The AI doesn't need any of it to help.

Can AI give kids wrong or inappropriate answers?

Yes. AI can make up facts and occasionally produce content that isn't right for kids. That's why using it together and talking about what you see matters.
