How do I make coloring pages for my kids with ChatGPT?

By Adam Hunter · October 18, 2026 · 3-minute read

Short answer: Tell the AI what your kid wants on the page and how old they are, then ask for thick black outlines on a plain white background with no shading. ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok can all make the picture, and you just download it and print. You don't need to upload a photo of your child. Describe them instead, like curly hair and a dinosaur T-shirt, and it still feels like them.

Prompt 1: the coloring page (paste into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok) 
Make a printable coloring page for a [age]-year-old.
Picture: [a friendly dinosaur having a picnic in a park].
Style rules:
- Black outlines only, on a plain white background.
- Thick, bold lines and big, simple shapes that are easy to color inside.
- No shading, no gray, no color fill, no tiny details.
- Leave plenty of white space.
- Portrait layout that fits on one US Letter page.
- [No words on the page / Add the word "[word]" in big outline letters at the top.]
Prompt 2: a "looks like my kid" character, no photo needed 
Add a cartoon kid to this coloring page who looks like my child, based only on this description:
[short curly hair, round glasses, a dinosaur T-shirt, rain boots, a big smile].
Keep it a simple cartoon, with the same thick black outlines and no shading.
Prompt 3: fix a page that won't color well 
Redo this coloring page. Make every line thicker and bolder, remove all gray and shading, make the shapes bigger and simpler for a [age]-year-old, and keep the background plain white.

How to do it

  1. Ask your kid what they want to color: a unicorn at the beach, a fire truck, the hero from tonight's bedtime story.
  2. Open an AI app that makes pictures: ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok. Claude can't make images.
  3. Paste Prompt 1 and fill in the [brackets].
  4. Look at the picture before your kid does. If the lines are thin or it added gray shading, paste Prompt 3.
  5. Download the image and print it with "fit to page" turned on.
  6. Making a stack for a party? Run the prompt a few times with different scenes. It's an easy table activity for a kid's birthday party.

Tips for pages that color well

  • Simpler for little ones: for ages 2 to 4, ask for "one big object, very few lines." For ages 8 and up, you can ask for a full scene or a pattern border.
  • Make a set: to keep the same character across pages, paste the same character description each time and change only the scene.
  • Free limits: free plans cap how many images you can make in a day, and the limits change. If you hit one, try again tomorrow or switch apps.
  • Seasonal pages: pumpkins, snowmen, or an elf to go with your Elf on the Shelf plan.

Skip the photo: Lots of posts online say to upload a photo of your child's face. You don't need to. A described cartoon is just as fun, and it keeps your kid's photo out of the AI app. If you want a real photo turned into a page, use one of the dog, a toy, or your house.

Safety first: Don't type your kids' full names, school, address, or photos into any AI tool. It doesn't need them. Most AI chatbots are for ages 13 and up (ChatGPT and Grok also need a parent's permission under 18), and Claude is 18+, so check each tool's age rules before a child uses it.

FAQ

Can ChatGPT turn a photo into a coloring page?

Yes, it can trace a photo you upload. We suggest not using photos of your kids. Describe them instead with Prompt 2, or use a photo of a pet, a favorite toy, or your house. See what kids should never share with AI.

Is it free?

Yes, with limits. The free versions of ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok can make images but cap how many you get per day. You don't need a paid plan for a few pages.

Which AI makes the best coloring pages?

Any of the three that make images will work if you use the style rules in Prompt 1. Results vary from one try to the next, so if a page comes out busy or shaded, use Prompt 3 rather than switching apps.

Can my kid make their own pages?

Make them together on your account. Most AI apps are for ages 13 and up. Let your kid pick the scene and describe it, and you do the typing.

Why did the AI add gray shading or color?

Image AIs like to add detail. Remind it of the rules: "black outlines only, no shading, no gray." Usually one reminder fixes it.
