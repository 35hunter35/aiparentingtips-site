How can my kid use ChatGPT for homework help without cheating?

By Adam Hunter · October 17, 2026 · 3-minute read

Short answer: Set the AI up as a tutor before your kid asks anything. The prompt below tells it to ask questions, give one hint at a time, and never just hand over the final answer. Sit with your child while they use it, and check the school's AI rules first.

Copy-paste prompt (works in free ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini) 
You are a friendly, patient tutor for a student in grade [grade].
The subject is [subject]. Your job is to help them learn, not to do the work.
Rules:
- Never give the final answer, even if asked. Give one small hint at a time.
- Start by asking what they already know and where they got stuck.
- Use simple words and short sentences suited to their grade.
- After each hint, ask them to try the next step themselves.
- When they get it right, ask them to explain why it works in their own words.
- If they seem frustrated, encourage them and break the step into something smaller.
The first problem is: [paste or type the problem]

How to do it

  1. Check your school's or teacher's rules on AI for homework.
  2. Open a free AI chatbot on your account, with your child next to you.
  3. Paste the tutor prompt first, before the homework question.
  4. Have your child type the problem and explain their thinking out loud.
  5. If the AI slips and gives the answer, type: "Remember, hints only."

Double-check: AI can make math and fact mistakes with total confidence. Have your child check answers against the textbook or class notes.

Safety first: Don't type your kids' full names, school, address, or photos into any AI tool. It doesn't need them. Most AI chatbots require users to be at least 13 (with a parent's permission under 18), and Claude requires users to be 18 or older, so check each tool's age rules before a child uses it.

FAQ

Is using ChatGPT for homework cheating?

It depends on how it's used and on the school's rules. Copying an answer is cheating. Using AI as a tutor that asks questions and gives hints is closer to having a patient helper at the kitchen table.

What age can kids use ChatGPT?

OpenAI requires users to be at least 13, with a parent's permission under 18. For younger kids, use it on your account while you sit with them.

Does this work for math?

Yes. The hint-by-hint approach works especially well for math, but AI can still make arithmetic mistakes, so check the final work.

Which AI is best for homework help?

The free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok all follow this prompt. Pick the one your family is comfortable with and stick to it.
