Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Short version: we collect your email address so we can send you the newsletter. We don't sell it, we don't give it to sponsors, and you can leave any time.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and anything else you choose to give us when you subscribe or reply to an email.
  • Basic newsletter stats, such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked. Our newsletter platform collects these so we can tell which tips are useful.

Who handles it

The newsletter is sent with beehiiv, which stores subscriber data for us. When you sign up through the form on this site, your email goes straight to beehiiv, and beehiiv's form may use cookies or similar tools to prevent spam. This website is hosted as plain static pages. It doesn't run its own analytics or set its own cookies. If that changes, we'll update this page.

Sponsors

Sponsors pay for a clearly labeled spot in the newsletter. They never receive your email address or personal details. If you click a sponsor's link, their own privacy policy applies on their site.

Kids

This newsletter is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our tips never ask you to share your children's personal details with us.

Your choices

  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • To see, correct, or delete your data, email hello@aiparentingtips.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
