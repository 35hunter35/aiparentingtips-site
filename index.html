Questions parents ask

Is it free?

Yes, completely. AI Parenting Tips is free and always will be. It's supported by a small number of carefully chosen sponsors, and every sponsored mention is clearly labeled.

Who writes it?

Adam Hunter, a dad of two who works in AI. Every prompt is tried at home with real kids, real homework, and real weeknight chaos before it goes out.

Which AI tools?

The prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini, including their free versions. When a tip only works well in one tool, we say so.

Is it safe for kids?

The tips are written for parents to use, and when kids are involved we suggest you sit with them. We never ask you to type your kids' full names, school, or other personal details into an AI tool, and each tip flags anything to double-check. Most AI chatbots require users to be at least 13, and some require 18, so check each tool's age rules.
