Questions parents ask

Is it free?

Yes, completely. AI Parenting Tips is free and always will be. It's supported by a small number of carefully chosen sponsors, and every sponsored mention is clearly labeled.

Who writes it?

Adam Hunter, a dad of two who builds with AI. Prompts are tried at home with real kids, real homework, and real weeknight chaos before they go out.

Which AI tools?

The prompts are written for ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and Claude, and their free versions work fine. Each tip says which tools it was tested in.

Is it safe for kids?

The tips are written for parents to use, and when kids are involved we suggest you sit with them. We never ask you to type your kids' full names, school, or other personal details into an AI tool, and each tip flags anything to double-check. Most AI chatbots are for ages 13 and up (ChatGPT and Grok also need a parent's permission under 18), and Claude is 18+, so check each tool's age rules.
